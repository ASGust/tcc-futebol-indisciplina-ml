================================================================================
2. REMOVAL OF REAL MATCHES WITHOUT STATISTICS
================================================================================

Real matches removed: 2

Removed matches:
league    season       date    home_team away_team  home_goals  away_goals
    F1 2016/2017 2017-04-16       Bastia      Lyon         0.0         3.0
    D1 2024/2025 2024-12-14 Union Berlin    Bochum         0.0         2.0

The decision to remove these matches was made because the impact is statistically negligible: 2 out of 18,011 rows, equivalent to roughly 0.011% of the base after removing the ghost rows.

These variables are central to the study, especially cards and fouls. No mean, median or zero imputation was used, since any of these alternatives would introduce an assumption about the discipline level of the matches without observed information that could methodologically support it.

Furthermore, keeping missing values in these matches could propagate the problem to Step 4, especially when building the sliding windows of 3 to 7 matches, where missing values in the middle of the window could prevent or distort the calculation of the historical features.

Therefore, the two real matches without statistics were removed, without imputing the missing values.
